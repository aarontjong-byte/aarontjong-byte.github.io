Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

TimeSheet is a private Windows desktop application operated by Aaron Tjong-A-Tjoe for his own business.

What data the app uses

With your authorization through Intuit's sign-in, the app reads employee, customer, service item and time activity records from your QuickBooks Online company, and creates time activities from the timesheet file you select.

Where data is stored

The app runs only on your own computer. It has no server and sends no data anywhere except directly to Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. Your QuickBooks connection details (company ID and authorization token) are saved in a settings file on your computer only.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with, or shown to anyone else.

Revoking access

You can disconnect the app at any time from your QuickBooks Online settings or the Intuit account portal.
